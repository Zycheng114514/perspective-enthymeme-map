# Who or what posts about {topic} are about
Each line is what one post's main point is about, in the post's own terms,
followed in brackets by that main point.
{items}

# Task
Bring together the lines that are about the same target.
- Keep the groups broad. A leader, the administration and agencies while
  that leader is in charge, and the decisions they take are one target. The
  officials, experts or bodies that they replaced or oppose are another. A
  requirement, product or programme together with its particular cases is
  one target.
- Keep a measure apart from what is done against it. A requirement is one
  target; the laws, orders and decisions that lift it, exempt people from
  it or make up for what it did to them are another target.
- Do not make a separate group for each decision, document or example; aim
  for groups that several posts share. A line that fits no shared group
  stays in a group of its own.
- Group each line by the words before the brackets: that is what the post
  is about. The main point in brackets is there only to show which people
  or bodies those words mean, because the same name can mean different
  people in different posts ("the CDC" or "health officials" under one
  leadership are not the same target as under another). Do not move a line
  to another target because its main point also mentions that target.
- about: a few words that name the target, clear enough to tell it from the
  other groups.
- ids: the ids of the lines in the group.
Do not sort by whether a post approves or disapproves. Put every id in
exactly one group.
