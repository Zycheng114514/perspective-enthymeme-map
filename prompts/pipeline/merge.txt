# Task
Several readers each laid out this post's reasoning. Each kept four things
apart: steps the post relies on to reach its point, what the post takes for
granted, where the post is heading beyond its point, and the reader's own
objections. Sort what they wrote, keeping those four apart as the readers did.
Do not judge who is right.

Group by what is claimed, not by wording. Readers phrase the same claim
differently, hedge it more or less ("can help", "would", "is likely to"), and
speak from their own point of view; none of that makes it a different claim.
Two items belong in one group if someone who accepted one would be accepting
the other. Keep two items apart only when they differ in substance, so that
one could hold while the other fails in a way that matters to the argument.

1. conclusions: group the readers' statements of the post's main point that
   say the same thing. For each group give one sentence that states the point
   itself, without the reasons for it (text), the wider claim it is an
   instance of (general_claim), and the readers who hold it. Ids are c1, c2, ...
2. premises: group the added steps (ids such as agrees.2) that make the same
   claim, across readers. Ids are g1, g2, ... For each group:
   - text: one sentence that states it plainly.
   - general_claim: see below.
   - kind:
     bridge: it links what the post states to the point.
     presupposition: something a stated reason takes for granted.
     value: a judgement about what is good, bad, or ought to be done.
     background: a factual claim about the world that the reasoning leans on.
     other: none of these.
   - members: the ids of the added steps in this group.
   - narrower_than: ids of groups that this one is a clearly narrower
     version of, where the difference matters to the argument.
   - conflicts_with: ids of groups that cannot both be what the post assumes.
3. granted: group the "takes for granted" items (ids such as agrees.t1) that
   say the same thing. Give text, general_claim and members. Ids are
   t1, t2, ...
4. further: group the "heading to" items (ids such as agrees.f1) that say the
   same thing. Give text, general_claim and members. Ids are f1, f2, ...
5. objections: group the objections (ids such as disagrees.o1) that say the
   same thing. Give text, general_claim and members. Ids are o1, o2, ...
6. restates: ids of added steps or "takes for granted" items that only repeat
   something the post itself states, or only repeat that reader's own
   statement of the main point. Leave these out of the groups.

general_claim, wherever it is asked for: the wider claim the item is an
instance of, stated without reference to this post or its author, short
enough to recur in other posts on the same topic. One claim only: do not join
two claims with "and", "so" or "because".

Every added step id must appear exactly once: in one premise group, or in
restates. Every "takes for granted" id must appear exactly once: in one
granted group, or in restates. Every "heading to" id must appear in exactly
one further group, and every objection id in exactly one objection group.
