# Task
The lines above are short claims collected from social media posts about
{topic}, grouped by post. {what} Different posts often get the same claim in
different words.

Draw up the list of distinct claims that occur in two or more posts. For each,
write one short, plain sentence that states it without reference to any
particular post.

- One assertion per claim. Do not join two assertions with "and", "so",
  "because" or "which shows". If posts assert A and also draw B from it, list
  A and B as two claims.
- Two lines are the same claim only if a careful reader would treat them as
  the same claim.
- A claim and its denial are different claims.
- A narrow claim and a much wider one are different claims.
- Leave out claims that occur in only one post.
