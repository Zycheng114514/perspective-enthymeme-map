# What posts about {topic} state and rely on
{claims}

# Task
The lines above come from social media posts about {topic}, grouped by post.
Each line is something the post states, or something it leaves unsaid and
relies on. Posts seldom repeat each other word for word. One names an agency
where another names a company; one cites an event where another cites a
number. Set such specifics aside and find the points the posts share.

List every general point that two or more posts make or rely on. Under each,
list the narrower versions of it that two or more posts share, if there are
any.

- A general point is one short, plain sentence that makes no reference to any
  particular post. Make it wide enough to cover the posts that share it, and
  no wider.
- A narrower version says something more specific than its general point: a
  particular kind of case, cause or consequence. Give none when the posts
  that share the point have nothing more specific in common.
- One assertion per sentence. Do not join two assertions with "and", "so",
  "because" or "which shows". If posts assert A and also draw B from it, list
  A and B as two points.
- Keep every point about {topic}. A sentence so wide that it no longer says
  anything about {topic} is too wide.
- A point and its denial are different points.
- Leave out anything only one post says.
