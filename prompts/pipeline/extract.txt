{post}

# Task
Record what this post states, in its own words. Do not fill anything in.

1. status
   - argument: the post gives at least one reason for a conclusion, or sets
     out facts or questions in a way that clearly points the reader toward a
     conclusion.
   - not_argument: an announcement, a greeting, a bare opinion with no
     reason, or a link with no comment.
   Judge by what is shown. A reply or a quote-post can be an argument even
   when the thing it answers is not shown.
   why: one short sentence giving the reason for the status.
   If the status is "not_argument", leave everything below empty.
2. context_missing: true if the post replies to or quotes something that is
   not shown and a reader would need it to be sure what the post means.
   Otherwise false.
3. arguments: the separate arguments the post makes. Most posts make one.
   List another only when the post argues for a different conclusion with
   reasons of its own; several reasons for one conclusion are one argument.
   Put the post's main argument first. Ids are A1, A2, ... For each:
   - reasons: each thing the post offers as a reason for this conclusion.
     Ids are r1, r2, ... within the argument.
     - span: the exact words, copied from the post.
     - says: what those words assert, as one plain sentence that can be
       understood without the post. Write it in the author's voice, without
       commentary on how the post uses it.
     - form: "rhetorical_question" if the words are a question that plainly
       implies an answer (write that answer in says); "sarcasm" if they mean
       the opposite of what they literally say (write what is meant);
       otherwise "plain".
     - general_claim: the wider claim this is an instance of, stated without
       reference to this post or its author, short enough to recur in other
       posts on the same topic. One claim only: do not join two claims with
       "and", "so" or "because".
   - conclusion_span: the exact words that state this argument's conclusion,
     if the post states it. Otherwise empty.
   - conclusion_says: what those words assert, as one plain sentence. Leave
     it empty if the post does not write the conclusion. Do not supply one.
   - conclusion_claim: the wider claim that stated conclusion is an instance
     of, stated without reference to this post or its author. One claim
     only. Empty if the post does not state the conclusion.
4. topic: vaccine, immigration, or other.
