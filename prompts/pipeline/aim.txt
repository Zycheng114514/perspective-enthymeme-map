# Task
Say who or what this argument is about, and which way the post leans.
- about: the person, institution, policy, product or group of people whose
  conduct, authority or worth the argument's main point is about. Name it in a
  few words, the way the post identifies it: a named official together with
  the body they lead, a particular requirement, a particular vaccine. If the
  main point touches several, name the one the post is chiefly judging. If
  it speaks in general, about no particular person, institution, policy,
  product or group, write "no one in particular".
- general: true only when about is "no one in particular".
- attitude: "backs" if the post defends or praises what it is about, or
  wants more of it; "opposes" if the post criticises or blames it, or wants
  it stopped, reversed or removed; "neither" if it does neither, or about is
  "no one in particular".
Go by what the post says. Do not guess who wrote it.
